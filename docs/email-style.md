# Archit's application-writing guide

This guide is for human authors and a future Application Agent. No AI generation
or email sending is implemented. Never invent experience, metrics, projects or
knowledge of a company. A saved draft is not approval to send it.

Tone: direct, confident, personal, slightly informal, specific and oriented toward
ownership. Avoid generic corporate cover letters, exaggerated praise, stock AI
phrases and unnecessary compression. Include enough relevant detail to be useful.

Subject: `Application for [Role] | Archit Singh Baghel`

- Greet the actual contact when known.
- Reference the source of the opportunity when helpful.
- Introduce Archit Singh, currently an SDE intern at Redseer Strategy Consultants.
- Explain why the specific role fits. Connect actual responsibilities to verified
  experience; do not merely list technologies or repeat the job description.
- Mention relevant Redseer work, IIT KGP work and freelance/client experience only
  when each helps explain the match.
- Include the explicitly selected resume's Drive link from `/resumes`.
- Invite a discussion and sign with the current profile values for full name,
  email, phone, portfolio, GitHub and LinkedIn.

## Technical profile

Emphasize backend/full-stack engineering, systems, shipping and end-to-end
ownership. Supported areas include FastAPI, Python, Node.js, React, TypeScript,
PostgreSQL/MySQL/MongoDB, Azure, Docker, CI/CD, AI/LLM systems, Agentic AI and RAG.
Connect relevant Redseer engineering, IIT Kharagpur backend work and freelance
engineering to the actual problem. Do not invent deliverables or performance
numbers; obtain project evidence before adding specifics.

## Product profile

Emphasize problem understanding, product discovery, stakeholder conversations,
requirements, ownership and shipped outcomes. Connect relevant Redseer product
ownership, IIT KGP field research and freelance client/product ownership to the
role. Technology understanding supports the product story. Do not reuse the
technical email with a changed subject line.

Use `/profile` as the form/signature source and `/resumes` as the link source so
edits stay authoritative in PostgreSQL. Unknown facts should remain unknown or
be requested from the user, never filled by model imagination.
